So where did the golden city come from? By Ibn Katheer's time, a rich legend had grown around the verse. In some versions, a king named Shaddaad, son of Aad, built it. Commentators described a city of bricks of gold and silver, its pebbles pearls and jewels, its soil made of musk. A city that moved from land to land: sometimes in Syria, sometimes in Yemen, sometimes in Iraq.
