Iram dhaat, al-Imaad. Translators still disagree on how to render it. Marmaduke Pickthall, in nineteen thirty, wrote many-columned Iram. A modern translation, Saheeh International, has Iram, who had lofty pillars. Even the next verse divides them. Was Iram a place, the like of which was never created? Or a people, the likes of whom?
