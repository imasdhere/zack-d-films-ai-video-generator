A translation is already an interpretation. Pickthall renders 89:7 as "many-columned Iram", which builds in one reading of *dhāt al-ʿimād*. Saheeh International renders 89:8 with "whom" (a tribe), Pickthall with "which". The film points this out instead of hiding it.

## What the Quran explicitly states (evidence level 1)

1. **Iram is named once**: 89:7, in apposition to ʿĀd (89:6), described as *dhāt al-ʿimād* ("possessing ʿimād": columns, poles, or supports) and as "the like of which was not created in the lands" (89:8).
2. ʿĀd were sent their "brother" Hūd (7:65; 11:50; 26:124; 46:21).
3. Hūd warned his people "at al-Aḥqāf" (46:21). Pickthall: "among the wind-curved sandhills".
4. ʿĀd came after the people of Noah and were "increased in stature" or "extent" (*basṭatan*, 7:69).
5. They built "on every high place a monument" (*āyah*) and took *maṣāniʿ* ("strongholds") (26:128–129).
6. They had cattle, sons, gardens and springs (26:133–134).
7. They asked, "Who is mightier than us in power?" (41:15).
8. They saw a cloud approaching their valleys and took it for rain; it was a wind bearing punishment (46:24–25). Afterwards "naught could be seen save their dwellings" (46:25).
9. The wind is called *ṣarṣar* ("raging", "roaring" or "cold-howling") and *ʿātiyah* (69:6), and *al-ʿaqīm* ("barren") (51:41). It lasted "seven nights and eight days" (69:7).
10. Hūd and those who believed with him were saved (7:72; 11:58).

## What the Quran does NOT state

- It does not give a location for Iram beyond the association of ʿĀd with **al-Aḥqāf** (46:21).
- It does not describe a city of gold or silver, a builder named Shaddād, a garden built to rival Paradise, or a city that moves or is hidden.
- It does not say the city was buried by sand. It says a wind destroyed the people and their dwellings remained visible (46:25).
- It gives no date.
- 89:7–8 does not specify whether *ʿimād* means stone columns, tent poles, or bodily stature. Exegetes disagree (see `islamic_sources.md`).

### Sūrat Fuṣṣilat 41:15–16 — 'Who is mightier than us in power?'; the raging wind

**41:15**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">فَأَمَّا عَادٌ فَٱسْتَكْبَرُوا۟ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ بِغَيْرِ ٱلْحَقِّ وَقَالُوا۟ مَنْ أَشَدُّ مِنَّا قُوَّةً ۖ أَوَلَمْ يَرَوْا۟ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَهُمْ هُوَ أَشَدُّ مِنْهُمْ قُوَّةً ۖ وَكَانُوا۟ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا يَجْحَدُونَ</p>

- Pickthall: As for A'ad, they were arrogant in the land without right, and they said: Who is mightier than us in power? Could they not see that Allah Who created them, He was mightier than them in power? And they denied Our revelations.

**41:16**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">فَأَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ رِيحًا صَرْصَرًا فِىٓ أَيَّامٍ نَّحِسَاتٍ لِّنُذِيقَهُمْ عَذَابَ ٱلْخِزْىِ فِى ٱلْحَيَوٰةِ ٱلدُّنْيَا ۖ وَلَعَذَابُ ٱلْـَٔاخِرَةِ أَخْزَىٰ ۖ وَهُمْ لَا يُنصَرُونَ</p>

- Pickthall: Therefor We let loose on them a raging wind in evil days, that We might make them taste the torment of disgrace in the life of the world. And verily the doom of the Hereafter will be more shameful, and they will not be helped.


### Sūrat al-Aḥqāf 46:21–26 — al-Aḥqāf; the cloud mistaken for rain; only dwellings remained

**46:21**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">۞ وَٱذْكُرْ أَخَا عَادٍ إِذْ أَنذَرَ قَوْمَهُۥ بِٱلْأَحْقَافِ وَقَدْ خَلَتِ ٱلنُّذُرُ مِنۢ بَيْنِ يَدَيْهِ وَمِنْ خَلْفِهِۦٓ أَلَّا تَعْبُدُوٓا۟ إِلَّا ٱللَّهَ إِنِّىٓ أَخَافُ عَلَيْكُمْ عَذَابَ يَوْمٍ عَظِيمٍ</p>

- Pickthall: And make mention (O Muhammad) of the brother of A'ad when he warned his folk among the wind-curved sandhills - and verily warners came and went before and after him - saying: Serve none but Allah. Lo! I fear for you the doom of a tremendous Day.

**46:22**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">قَالُوٓا۟ أَجِئْتَنَا لِتَأْفِكَنَا عَنْ ءَالِهَتِنَا فَأْتِنَا بِمَا تَعِدُنَآ إِن كُنتَ مِنَ ٱلصَّٰدِقِينَ</p>

- Pickthall: They said: Hast come to turn us away from our gods? Then bring upon us that wherewith thou threatenest us, if thou art of the truthful.

**46:23**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">قَالَ إِنَّمَا ٱلْعِلْمُ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ وَأُبَلِّغُكُم مَّآ أُرْسِلْتُ بِهِۦ وَلَٰكِنِّىٓ أَرَىٰكُمْ قَوْمًا تَجْهَلُونَ</p>

- Pickthall: He said: The knowledge is with Allah only. I convey unto you that wherewith I have been sent, but I see you are a folk that know not.

**46:24**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">فَلَمَّا رَأَوْهُ عَارِضًا مُّسْتَقْبِلَ أَوْدِيَتِهِمْ قَالُوا۟ هَٰذَا عَارِضٌ مُّمْطِرُنَا ۚ بَلْ هُوَ مَا ٱسْتَعْجَلْتُم بِهِۦ ۖ رِيحٌ فِيهَا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ</p>

- Pickthall: Then, when they beheld it as a dense cloud coming toward their valleys, they said: Here is a cloud bringing us rain. Nay, but it is that which ye did seek to hasten, a wind wherein is painful torment,

**46:25**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">تُدَمِّرُ كُلَّ شَىْءٍۭ بِأَمْرِ رَبِّهَا فَأَصْبَحُوا۟ لَا يُرَىٰٓ إِلَّا مَسَٰكِنُهُمْ ۚ كَذَٰلِكَ نَجْزِى ٱلْقَوْمَ ٱلْمُجْرِمِينَ</p>

- Pickthall: Destroying all things by commandment of its Lord. And morning found them so that naught could be seen save their dwellings. Thus do We reward the guilty folk.

**46:26**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">وَلَقَدْ مَكَّنَّٰهُمْ فِيمَآ إِن مَّكَّنَّٰكُمْ فِيهِ وَجَعَلْنَا لَهُمْ سَمْعًا وَأَبْصَٰرًا وَأَفْـِٔدَةً فَمَآ أَغْنَىٰ عَنْهُمْ سَمْعُهُمْ وَلَآ أَبْصَٰرُهُمْ وَلَآ أَفْـِٔدَتُهُم مِّن شَىْءٍ إِذْ كَانُوا۟ يَجْحَدُونَ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُوا۟ بِهِۦ يَسْتَهْزِءُونَ</p>

- Pickthall: And verily We had empowered them with that wherewith We have not empowered you, and had assigned them ears and eyes and hearts; but their ears and eyes and hearts availed them naught since they denied the revelations of Allah; and what they used to mock befell them.


### Sūrat al-Dhāriyāt 51:41–42 — the barren wind

**51:41**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">وَفِى عَادٍ إِذْ أَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمُ ٱلرِّيحَ ٱلْعَقِيمَ</p>

- Pickthall: And in (the tribe of) A'ad (there is a portent) when we sent the fatal wind against them.

**51:42**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">مَا تَذَرُ مِن شَىْءٍ أَتَتْ عَلَيْهِ إِلَّا جَعَلَتْهُ كَٱلرَّمِيمِ</p>

- Pickthall: It spared naught that it reached, but made it (all) as dust.

### Sūrat al-Qamar 54:18–21 — wind on a day of continuous calamity

**54:18**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">كَذَّبَتْ عَادٌ فَكَيْفَ كَانَ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ</p>

- Pickthall: (The tribe of) A'ad rejected warnings. Then how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings.

**54:19**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">إِنَّآ أَرْسَلْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ رِيحًا صَرْصَرًا فِى يَوْمِ نَحْسٍ مُّسْتَمِرٍّ</p>

- Pickthall: Lo! We let loose on them a raging wind on a day of constant calamity,

**54:20**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">تَنزِعُ ٱلنَّاسَ كَأَنَّهُمْ أَعْجَازُ نَخْلٍ مُّنقَعِرٍ</p>

- Pickthall: Sweeping men away as though they were uprooted trunks of palm-trees.

**54:21**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">فَكَيْفَ كَانَ عَذَابِى وَنُذُرِ</p>

- Pickthall: Then see how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings!


### Sūrat al-Ḥāqqa 69:4–8 — seven nights and eight days

**69:4**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">كَذَّبَتْ ثَمُودُ وَعَادٌۢ بِٱلْقَارِعَةِ</p>

- Pickthall: (The tribes of) Thamud and A'ad disbelieved in the judgment to come.

**69:5**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">فَأَمَّا ثَمُودُ فَأُهْلِكُوا۟ بِٱلطَّاغِيَةِ</p>

- Pickthall: As for Thamud, they were destroyed by the lightning.

**69:6**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">وَأَمَّا عَادٌ فَأُهْلِكُوا۟ بِرِيحٍ صَرْصَرٍ عَاتِيَةٍ</p>

- Pickthall: And as for A'ad, they were destroyed by a fierce roaring wind,

**69:7**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">سَخَّرَهَا عَلَيْهِمْ سَبْعَ لَيَالٍ وَثَمَٰنِيَةَ أَيَّامٍ حُسُومًا فَتَرَى ٱلْقَوْمَ فِيهَا صَرْعَىٰ كَأَنَّهُمْ أَعْجَازُ نَخْلٍ خَاوِيَةٍ</p>

- Pickthall: Which He imposed on them for seven long nights and eight long days so that thou mightest have seen men lying overthrown, as they were hollow trunks of palm-trees.

**69:8**

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">فَهَلْ تَرَىٰ لَهُم مِّنۢ بَاقِيَةٍ</p>

- Pickthall: Canst thou (O Muhammad) see any remnant of them?
